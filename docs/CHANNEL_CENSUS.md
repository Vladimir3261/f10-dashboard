`distinct %` is the share of stored rows carrying a value not already seen.
Low is not automatically wrong - `gear` *should* repeat, and a constant
`park_state` is a real observation. It measures where sampling effort goes.

## What it shows

  1.73M rows (83%), at 0.1-3.8% distinct.
- **`egs_da2e_b0` is the clearest waste**: 124,485 rows, ONE distinct value.
  Now `log: false` - decoded and displayed, never stored.
- **The proprietary DDE channels are the best value**: ~1,100 rows each at
  0.09 Hz, many 20-75% distinct. `n47d_exh_temp_pre_cat` is 75% distinct in
  1,113 rows; `lambda` is 0.5% distinct in 129,781.
- **`runtime` at 62% distinct** is expected - it is a monotonic clock.
- **`error %` is 0 everywhere because fault recording only just landed.**
  Until now `on_error` went nowhere, so a request that timed out or was
  refused left no trace - indistinguishable from a channel nobody asked
  about. The column fills from the next drive; a channel with a real error
  rate is the signal to look for.
