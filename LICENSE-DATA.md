# Scope of the licence: code, not data

`LICENSE` (MIT) covers the CODE in this repository: the runtime, the mapping
engine, the tools, the tests and the documentation written for them.

It does NOT relicense the vehicle DATA. The mapping files under mappings/
and the research records under research/ are built from cited facts whose
sources carry their own terms; each fact is labelled with its origin and
each source is pinned with its licence in research/manifests/sources.yaml
and the register in research/reports/legal-and-license-notes.md. Bulk
material from a source that forbids or leaves open redistribution is not
in this tree (research/normalized/README.md), and THIRD_PARTY.md says what
was used and how. Reuse the data on those terms, not on this one.
